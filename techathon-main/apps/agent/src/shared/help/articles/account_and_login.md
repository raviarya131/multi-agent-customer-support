# Account & Login

## I forgot my password
On the login screen, choose **Forgot password** and follow the email link to set
a new one. The link is valid for a limited time — request a fresh one if it
expires.

## How do I change my email address?
Open **Settings → Account → Email**, enter the new address, and confirm via the
verification link we send to it.

## Why can't I log in?
The most common causes are a mistyped password or using a different email than
the one on the account. If you're sure they're correct and still can't get in,
start a support chat so an agent can check your account.

## How do I keep my account secure?
Use a unique password and don't share your login. We'll never ask for your
password by email or chat.
