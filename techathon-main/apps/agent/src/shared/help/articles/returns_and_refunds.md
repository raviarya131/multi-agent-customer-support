# Returns & Refunds

## How long do I have to request a refund?
You can request a refund within **30 days** of the original charge date. Charges
older than 30 days need a quick manual review by our billing team.

## I was charged twice — can I get the duplicate back?
Yes. If you were charged more than once for the same item in the same billing
cycle, the **duplicate charge is always fully refundable**, even outside the
30-day window.

## What can't be refunded?
One-time setup fees and usage-based overage charges are non-refundable once the
billing cycle has closed.

## How long does a refund take?
Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within
**5–7 business days**.
