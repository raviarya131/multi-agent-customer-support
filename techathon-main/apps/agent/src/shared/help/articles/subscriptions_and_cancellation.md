# Subscriptions & Cancellation

## How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. You can cancel anytime — no phone
call or support ticket required.

## When does cancellation take effect?
At the **end of your current billing cycle**. You keep access to paid features
until that date and you won't be charged again.

## Is there a notice period?
Monthly plans have no notice period. Annual plans require **30 days' notice**
before the renewal date to avoid auto-renewing for the next year.

## Does cancelling give me a refund?
Cancelling on its own doesn't refund the current cycle. Refund eligibility is
governed by our refund policy (for example, duplicate charges remain
refundable).

## Can I reactivate after cancelling?
Yes. You can reactivate before the cycle ends with no loss of data. After the
cycle ends, your data is retained for **90 days** before deletion.
