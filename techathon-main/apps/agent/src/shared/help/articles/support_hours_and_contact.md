# Support Hours & Contact

## When is support available?
Our human support team is available **Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm**. Outside those
hours you can still start a chat — the assistant answers common questions
instantly, and anything that needs a person is queued for the next business day.

## How do I reach a human agent?
If the self-service answers don't solve your problem, choose **Talk to support**
to start a conversation. Your question is routed to the right specialist team
automatically.

## How quickly will I get a response?
Response times depend on the priority of the issue. Urgent, service-impacting
problems are handled first; general questions are answered in order. You'll get
a notification as soon as an agent replies.
