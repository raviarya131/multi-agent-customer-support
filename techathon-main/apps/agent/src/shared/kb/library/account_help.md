# Account Access Help

## Locked accounts
An account is locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. A locked
account auto-unlocks after 30 minutes, or the customer can reset their password
to unlock it immediately.

## Password reset
Customers reset their password from the "Forgot password" link on the login
screen. The reset email is valid for 60 minutes. Resetting the password also
clears a lockout.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
If MFA is enabled and the customer lost access to their authenticator, they must
verify identity via the backup email before MFA can be reset. Support cannot
disable MFA without identity verification.

## Last login
If a customer reports they "can't get in" but the account is not locked and a
recent successful login exists, the most likely cause is a wrong password or a
cached session — advise a password reset and a fresh browser session.

## Data safety
Login and access issues never affect stored data. Reassure customers their data
is intact while access is being restored.
