# Cancellation Policy

## How to cancel
Customers can cancel a subscription at any time from Settings > Billing >
Cancel plan. No phone call or support ticket is required.

## When cancellation takes effect
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. The customer
keeps access to paid features until that date and is not charged again.

## Notice period
Monthly plans have no notice period. Annual plans require 30 days' notice before
the renewal date to avoid auto-renewal for the next year.

## Refunds on cancellation
Cancelling does not by itself trigger a refund for the current cycle. Refund
eligibility for the current cycle is governed by the refund policy
(e.g. duplicate charges remain refundable).

## Reactivation
A cancelled subscription can be reactivated before the end of the cycle with no
loss of data. After the cycle ends, data is retained for 90 days before
deletion.
