# Product Catalog Guidance

## Availability and stock
Stock levels and prices shown to customers must come from the catalog lookup,
never estimated. An item is purchasable only when it is marked in stock. When an
item is out of stock, share the restock ETA from the catalog record if present,
and offer to note the customer's interest.

## Variants
Many products have variants (size, colour, switch type, capacity). Only the
variants listed in the catalog record are available — do not imply a size or
colour exists if it is not listed.

## Specifications and warranty
Specifications, materials, and warranty terms come from the product's spec
fields. If a requested detail is not in the record, say it is not available
rather than estimating, and offer to connect the customer with the team.

## Recommendations
Recommendations and comparisons must be based only on listed products and their
stated specs. Do not invent benchmarks, reviews, or claims that are not in the
catalog data.

## Pricing
Prices are shown in the catalog currency. Promotional pricing, coupons, and
bundle discounts are applied at checkout and are outside catalog lookups; if a
customer asks about a discount not reflected in the catalog, direct them to the
active offers on the product page.
