# Refund Policy

## Eligibility window
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original charge date.
Charges older than 30 days require manual review by the billing team.

## Duplicate charges
If a customer was charged more than once for the same item in the same billing
cycle, the duplicate charge is always eligible for a full refund regardless of
the 30-day window.

## Non-refundable items
One-time setup fees and usage-based overage charges are non-refundable once the
billing cycle has closed.

## Processing time
Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
