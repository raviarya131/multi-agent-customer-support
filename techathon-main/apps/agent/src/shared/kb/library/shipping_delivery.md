# Shipping & Delivery

## Order statuses
- **processing**: payment confirmed, warehouse is preparing the order. Not yet shipped.
- **shipped**: handed to the carrier; a tracking number is available.
- **out_for_delivery**: with the local courier for delivery that day.
- **delivered**: marked delivered by the carrier.
- **delayed**: in transit but past the original estimate; a revised ETA applies.
- **cancelled**: the order was cancelled and will not ship.

## Delivery estimates
Standard delivery is 3–5 business days after dispatch. Estimated delivery dates
shown to customers come from the carrier and can shift; always quote the ETA
from the order record, never a guessed date.

## Delayed orders
If an order is delayed, acknowledge it, share the revised ETA from the order
record, and reassure the customer it is still in transit. Orders delayed more
than 7 days past the original ETA, or showing no carrier movement for 5+ days,
should be raised with Order Operations to open a carrier investigation.

## Lost or missing parcels
A parcel marked "delivered" but not received should be checked with neighbours
and the delivery location first. If still missing after 48 hours, Order
Operations opens a lost-parcel claim with the carrier.

## Tracking
Tracking numbers become available once an order is shipped. Customers can track
via the carrier's site using the tracking number on the order record.
